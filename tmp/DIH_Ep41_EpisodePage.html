Questions Answered

What is healing with horses?

Healing with horses is ground-based work where a person spends time with a horse and pays attention to how the horse responds. Dr. Kimberly Ammerman of Healing Hearts with Horses says horses are grounded and mirror a person's energy back to them. She coaches people through what they notice. Nobody rides.

What do horses pick up on in people?

Dr. Kimberly Ammerman says the top thing is energy. Horses stand on four feet, eat grass and live in the sun, so they are very grounded. People are busy and scattered. In her experience an anxious person can send a horse running around the round pen, while a calm person may get walked right up to.

Do you ride the horses at Healing Hearts with Horses?

No. Dr. Kimberly Ammerman says everything is done on the ground. A session can be as simple as grooming a horse while she asks questions, watches and gives space. She says the quiet moments are often where a person gets what she calls the aha moment. No riding skill is needed.

Is equine-assisted coaching the same as equine therapy?

No. Dr. Kimberly Ammerman is a coach, not a therapist. Her work at Healing Hearts with Horses is equine-assisted coaching and personal development. It is not psychotherapy, counseling or medical care. She is also a practicing pharmacist and says medicine has its place. Coaching does not replace care from a licensed provider.

Do I need horse experience, and what if I am afraid of horses?

You do not need any horse knowledge and you do not need to know how to ride. Dr. Kimberly Ammerman works with horse people and with people who are scared of horses. One woman arrived scared to death of them and left kissing a horse on the cheek. Dr. Kimberly says you just need to come and be open.

Who is this kind of work for?

Dr. Kimberly Ammerman says people of all ages come, often when they feel stuck and are ready for something to change. Some are carrying grief. Some are searching for their next step. She has seen children who felt unheard open up around a horse. It helps to be open and growth oriented.

What should I wear to an equine coaching session or retreat?

Closed-toe shoes are the one requirement. No flip-flops. Horses are big and can step on a foot by accident, and the ranch is natural ground in the Black Hills. Dr. Kimberly Ammerman says fancy boots are not required and anybody is welcome. Cell service is limited, so plan to be off your phone.

How do I become an equine coach?

Dr. Kimberly Ammerman teaches a three-level program. Level one builds the foundation. Level two covers how to facilitate: how to be curious, ask questions and help people. Level three is mastery. Her first class had eight people, including students from Australia and Sweden. Details are at healingheartswithhorses.com.

Where is Healing Hearts with Horses?

Healing Hearts with Horses is on 100 acres in the Black Hills near Whitewood, South Dakota, close to Deadwood. Dr. Kimberly Ammerman keeps 17 horses there and holds two, three and five day retreats plus private sessions. The work happens outdoors. She does not use a barn. Find her at healingheartswithhorses.com.
